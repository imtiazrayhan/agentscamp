The best AI video generator in 2026 depends on whether you want a model or a platform. [Kling](/tools/kling) has the deepest native specs from one vendor: Kling 4.0 generates 30-second clips with audio, Omni reference, and keyframes, and the 3.0 series does native 4K with multilingual dialogue. [Runway](/tools/runway) is the broadest platform: its own Gen-4.5, Aleph 2.0 editing, Act-Two capture, 54 hosted models including Kling 3.0, Adobe plugins, MCP, and an API. Google's Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni Flash are the audio-first picks in Google's apps and API, Luma's Ray3.2 is built for post-production, MiniMax H3 and Wan2.2 are the open-weight options, Pika is an app layer over a hundred models, and OpenAI's Sora is discontinued. Prices are left to the tool pages.

## Runway: the platform

Runway's own flagship is Gen-4.5 (December 1, 2025), which the company says "ranked #1 on the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video benchmark with 1,247 Elo points" at launch, a vendor-reported figure; native audio arrived that December and image-to-video in January 2026. The API spec is modest next to Kling, 2 to 10 seconds at 720p with 4K upscaling in the app. Aleph 2.0 lets you "edit one frame" and have the model modify "the rest of your video to match" on clips up to 30 seconds at 1080p; Act-Two tracks face, body, and hands; GWM-1 runs to two minutes at 720p; and the catalog lists 54 models from Alibaba, ByteDance, Google, Kling, MiniMax, OpenAI, and xAI. MCP is on the Pro and Max plans, and plugins run inside Premiere Pro and After Effects since September 8, 2026.

**Verdict:** the pick for a team that wants one subscription, one API, and one timeline across several vendors' models, plus real editing tools.

## Kling: the deepest single-vendor model

Kling 3.0 arrived in January 2026 with "extended video duration of up to 15 seconds, and native audio generation across multiple languages, dialects, and accents," plus an Omni variant that takes "visual traits and voice characteristics of a character" from a reference video; native 4K followed in April, which Kling called a world first. Kling 4.0 (October 15, 2026) "supports dynamic motions, Omni reference, native 30s-generation and multiple keyframes," though the vendor has published few other specs and the API does not yet support it. Kling runs on web, mobile, and desktop, sells the 3.0 series through its API, and launched an MCP server and a CLI in mid-2026. The catch is the user policy, covered below.

**Verdict:** the pick for the longest, sharpest native clips with audio from one model family, if the terms fit your use.

## Google Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni Flash

Google now has two video stories. Veo 3.1 "is a model for generating 8-second videos (720p, 1080p, or 4k) with natively generated audio," and can "extend videos that you previously generated with Veo by 7 seconds and up to 20 times" to 148 seconds at 720p. The Gemini API docs now recommend Gemini Omni Flash as "your default model for video generation," keeping Veo 3.1 "for specific capabilities like scene extension, last-frame control, or integration with legacy pipelines"; Omni adds multi-turn editing, subject references, and extension to 40 seconds. As of October 2026 the deprecations page gives the Veo 3.1 preview IDs a shutdown date of October 22, 2026, while the GA Veo 3.1 models continue on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, the renamed Vertex AI.

**Verdict:** the pick for audio-first clips inside Google's apps or the Gemini API, with Veo for extension pipelines and Omni for conversational editing.

## Luma Ray3.2: the post-production model

Luma released Ray3.2 on June 9, 2026, with "the full control surface of the Ray model" available as an API for the first time. The product page is aimed at editors: Multi-Keyframe "lets you set up to 16 keyframes inside a single clip," Modify Video swaps "the wall, the world, the wardrobe" on clips "up to 20 seconds, all at 1080p," and output includes "16-bit EXR frames for color grading, compositing, and VFX." Text- and image-to-video clips are 5 or 10 seconds, and the FAQ is explicit that "there's no natively-generated audio." Its terms say "Customer owns and retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Output."

**Verdict:** the pick for VFX and color pipelines that need keyframe control and EXR output, and can add sound in the edit.

## Hailuo and MiniMax H3: open weights with a stereo soundtrack

MiniMax's video model is MiniMax H3, open-sourced on August 3, 2026: a "33B-parameter dense, single-stream Transformer" that "can generate video with native stereo audio at resolutions up to 2K and durations of up to 15 seconds," with dialogue in 11 languages. The API offers H3 and a faster H3 Max, with reference inputs of up to nine images, three videos, and three audio clips; two modules stay closed. The consumer app is Hailuo. The weights ship under the MiniMax H3 Community License: "MiniMax claims no rights over the Outputs you generate," but it excludes "the European Union, the United Kingdom, the Republic of Korea and the United States of America" and requires that you "prominently display 'MiniMax H3'" in a commercial product. The consumer app's terms could not be read, so hosted-use rules are not stated by the vendor.

**Verdict:** the pick for stereo dialogue and reference-heavy clips, and for self-hosting outside the excluded territories.

## Pika: apps over models

Pika relaunched on September 17, 2026 as "the new Pika," with "the world's leading gen media models underneath, and focused creative apps on top." Video Studio lets you "attach up to 50 characters, products, settings, and styles" and "create a short clip or a multi-shot video up to 30 seconds long, with sound," from a roster including Seedance, MiniMax H3, Veo, Kling, and Pika's own Pika 2.5. An API Club resells "100+ leading video, image, audio, and LLM models through one API," and a ChatGPT plugin arrived October 16, 2026. The terms say "Pika does not claim ownership" of outputs, but the service "is for your personal, noncommercial use only" unless your plan includes commercial rights.

**Verdict:** the pick for marketers who want guided apps and many models in one place.

## Wan: the open weights that stopped at 2.2

Wan2.2 (July 28, 2025) ships 14-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts models and a 5-billion model that "supports both text-to-video and image-to-video generation at 720P resolution with 24fps," generating silent 5-second clips under Apache-2.0, with a README stating "we claim no rights over the your generated contents." The 2026 open releases, Wan-Dancer and Wan-Animate-2, are specialists. The flagships are not open. Wan2.5 through 3.0 exist only on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio and in the wan.video app; Wan3.0 "can generate videos up to 30 seconds long at 30fps" with audio on by default and a watermark off by default, and as of October 2026 its API reference marks it "currently in preview." No repository above the 2.2 family existed on GitHub or Hugging Face as of October 2026; Alibaba has said nothing either way.

**Verdict:** the pick for self-hosting and ComfyUI pipelines on Wan2.2, and the API pick for 30-second clips with audio on Wan3.0.

## Other 2026 entrants: Seedance, Grok Imagine, HappyHorse

ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 is "built for 30-second storytelling with precise reference control," generating "up to 30 seconds in a single generation, with the option to extend twice," resold inside Luma, Pika, and Runway; its resolution and release date are not stated by the vendor. xAI's grok-imagine-video-1.5 "generates lip-synced speech, renders text-to-video and image-to-video at native 1080p" for 1 to 15 seconds through the Imagine API. Alibaba's HappyHorse 1.1 "supports 1080P resolution and clips up to 15 seconds long" and is now Alibaba's recommended short-clip model. Midjourney's video stays at its 2025 V1 model, so [Midjourney](/tools/midjourney) belongs in the image guide.

## What happened to Sora

OpenAI's help center answers directly: "The Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on April 26, 2026," and "The Sora API will be discontinued on September 24, 2026." The API docs now read: "The Sora 2 models and Videos API were shut down on September 24, 2026 and are no longer available. No one-to-one replacement API is available." Developers were notified on March 24, 2026, and creators could export from the sunset page. Sora 2 had launched on September 30, 2025 with an invite-based iOS app and 20-second clips at up to 1080p. Reasons reported elsewhere are not confirmed on openai.com.

## Licensing, watermarks and ownership

Read the terms before client work, because the vendors do not agree.

Runway says "The Company does not claim ownership of any of your Inputs or Outputs" and "does not restrict your commercial use of your Outputs," and in the same document that "Inputs and Outputs may be used by the Company to train and improve its AI models." "No watermarks" is a paid-plan feature.

Kling says "You own all intellectual property rights and other proprietary interests in and to the Content under applicable law," but section 4.5 requires you to "label the Output interface with the brand and logo involved (including but not limited to 'Kling AI')" absent written permission, and section 4.6 says "without our written permission, you may not use, reproduce, distribute, and create derivative works of, and make modifications to, the Output for any commercial purposes." Paid plans then grant commercial use and watermark removal.

Google's terms say "Google won't claim ownership over that content"; "All generated videos include SynthID watermarking," and consumer outputs from the Gemini app, Flow, and YouTube also carry C2PA credentials. Luma's customer "owns and retains all right, title, and interest" in output. Pika claims no ownership but gates commercial use by plan. MiniMax's weights license requires attribution and excludes four territories. Wan2.2 is Apache-2.0. No vendor here warrants that an output is original or non-infringing; that risk stays with you.

## How to choose

Product and social clips with sound: Kling 3.0 or 4.0 for depth, or Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni Flash if the team lives in Google's apps. Editing footage you already have: Runway's Aleph 2.0 or Luma's Modify Video, and for caption-and-cut work without generation, [VEED](/tools/veed). A 30-second story in one pass: Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5, or Wan3.0 by API. Generation inside your own product: Runway's API across 54 models, the Gemini API, or MiniMax's platform. Self-hosting: Wan2.2 anywhere, or MiniMax H3 outside the excluded territories. Color and VFX: Luma for EXR, Runway's Max plan for ProRes 4444. Guided apps for non-specialists: Pika or Google Flow.
