**The best ChatGPT alternative in 2026 depends on what you use ChatGPT for.** For writing and careful work over your own files, [Claude](/tools/claude) is the strongest replacement. For research you need to cite, [Perplexity](/tools/perplexity) puts inline citations on every answer. If you want GPT, Claude and Gemini models without three subscriptions, [ChatLLM](/tools/chatllm) from Abacus.AI puts them in one app. If your company runs on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, [Gemini](/tools/gemini) or [Microsoft Copilot](/tools/microsoft-copilot) is already inside the apps you use. [DeepSeek](/tools/deepseek) is the free app with open weights, [Grok](/tools/grok) the one for live news from X, and [Mistral Vibe](/tools/mistral-vibe), formerly Le Chat, the European option that bundles chat with a coding agent. For privacy, Duck.ai from DuckDuckGo lets you use several models anonymously. This guide quotes no prices; each tool page lists current plans with dates.

## What you are replacing: ChatGPT in November 2026

Since the GPT-6 rollout began on October 7, 2026, ChatGPT's Chat tab runs on GPT-6 Sol for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise and on GPT-6 Luna for Free and Go, with GPT-6 Astra behind Pro's reasoning option; GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are in preview in Work and Codex as of November 2026. Around the models sit deep research, Projects, scheduled tasks, custom GPTs, always-on agents called dots on Pro and Business Premium, and Space, a pages-and-slides feature that "replaces Library." Plans run Free, Go, Plus, Pro (now with "your choice of 3 usage tiers"), Business, Enterprise and Edu.

Three details matter if you are weighing a switch. Go "may include ads." Free has limited memory and deep research. And for services for individuals, OpenAI says "we may use your content to train our models" unless you opt out; Temporary Chat is excluded, and business products are not trained on by default. If none of that bothers you, ChatGPT remains a strong default, and the [ChatGPT page](/tools/chatgpt) covers it in full. Each alternative below beats it at something specific.

## Claude: the best alternative for writing

Claude runs on the web, macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, with a Linux desktop app in beta as of November 2026. Free gets the Sonnet and Haiku models; Opus starts at Pro. What makes Claude a replacement rather than a second opinion is the workspace around the chat: Projects (up to five on Free), Artifacts on every plan, and Claude Design, Slides and Docs listed on Free too, along with memory, connectors, skills and incognito chats. Since September 16, 2026, "Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude," so paid plans can hand off and schedule tasks that work "across your files and tools," and Claude in Chrome, on paid plans, "clicks, types, and fills forms while you decide what happens next."

The limits: Free has "limited usage every 5 hours and every week," and Claude Code, Claude in Chrome and Research are paid-only. Consumer chats may be used to improve models when the Model Improvement setting is on; incognito chats are not, and Team and Enterprise list no model training by default.

**Verdict:** the best all-round replacement, and the pick for long-form writing and delegated work over your own files.

## Gemini: the pick for Google Workspace

The Gemini app moved to the Gemini 4 family this year: Hydrogen, Neon ("frontier-level intelligence") and Carbon ("our strongest model"). Without a paid plan you get Auto, which picks "Hydrogen or a fast, lightweight model"; Google AI Plus includes Hydrogen, Pro adds Neon, and Ultra, now in two tiers, adds Carbon. The Free plan lists Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas and Skills, which Google says "will soon replace Gems." Paid plans put Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Vids, and [Gemini Notebook](/tools/notebooklm) sits inside the app. Gemini runs on the web, Android and iOS, and since April and September 2026 on macOS and Windows.

On privacy, Google asks you not to "enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see," because human reviewers read a subset of chats, and activity auto-deletes after 18 months by default.

**Verdict:** the natural replacement if your documents and email live in Google's apps, with a free plan that includes Deep Research.

## Microsoft Copilot: the pick for Microsoft 365

From August 2026, Microsoft merged its consumer Copilot app and the Microsoft 365 Copilot app into one app called Microsoft Copilot, an assistant "for work and life." With a personal Microsoft account, "you can continue to chat with Copilot, create images, upload files and more for free, subject to available capacity and limits." Paid consumer plans are Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium and Pro. Cowork, an agent that creates Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files, sends email and manages your calendar, is a Premium or Pro feature in preview as of November 2026. On the Family plan, AI is for the subscription owner only.

Microsoft says prompts, responses and file contents in the Microsoft 365 apps for home "aren't used to train foundation models." The [Microsoft 365 Copilot page](/tools/microsoft-copilot) covers the work licence that runs in the same app with a work account, and [Microsoft 365 Copilot vs ChatGPT for Work](/guides/comparisons/microsoft-365-copilot-vs-chatgpt-for-work) is the head-to-head for teams.

**Verdict:** the replacement for people whose files live in Word, Excel and Outlook, and a usable free assistant for anyone with a Microsoft account.

## Perplexity: research you can cite

Perplexity is an answer engine: "Every answer includes inline citations that show exactly where each claim comes from." It "orchestrates multiple top models and selects the best one per query, including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, and NVIDIA Nemotron," and Pro and Max let you choose a preferred model. Pro adds Perplexity Computer, "premium data sources like PitchBook and Statista," and a model council, and Automations and Voice mode arrived in October and November 2026. The free tier is "Search only … Better for simple questions … Access to basic AI models." Because it starts from search rather than drafting, a common pattern is to gather and check facts in Perplexity and then write elsewhere; the [document research with citations workflow](/guides/workflow/ai-document-research-with-citations) keeps sources attached through that hand-off.

**Verdict:** the best ChatGPT alternative for research you will quote.

## ChatLLM: many models in one subscription

[ChatLLM](https://chatllm.abacus.ai), from Abacus.AI, is the option for people who don't want to pick one lab. A single subscription covers GPT 6.2 Sol and Astra, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2, Gemini 4 Neon, Grok 4.7, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi and GLM models; you choose one from the selector or leave it on RouteLLM, which Abacus.AI says "automatically routes each prompt to the best model for the job." Around the chat sit image and video generation, document and data analysis, web search, Deep Research, Projects, connectors to Slack, Teams, Gmail and Google Drive, and Abacus AI Agent, formerly DeepAgent, which "builds and deploys full-stack web and mobile apps." Paid plans can import your ChatGPT and Claude conversations without spending credits, which makes the switch easier. Abacus.AI says it does not train on your prompts or uploads, and that it has a zero data retention agreement with every model provider in the product. The trade-offs: there is no free plan, and usage is measured in monthly credits with no published per-model rate for chat.

**Verdict:** the pick if you want GPT, Claude and Gemini models side by side without paying for each.

## Grok: live news from X

Grok comes from SpaceXSI (SpaceX Super Intelligence, formerly xAI); SpaceX announced it had acquired xAI on February 2, 2026. Its distinguishing feature is live search across the web and X, built for "breaking news and trends," alongside multi-agent answers, Imagine for images and short videos, and Automations. Since October 12, 2026 it has been sold through the X Subscription, from Free to Ultra, which bundles Grok with X Premium features and Cursor; SuperGrok no longer appears on the pricing page. Its own FAQ carries the caveats: Grok "may at times include in its responses misleading or factually incorrect information," and it may train on your conversations unless you switch off "Improve the model."

**Verdict:** the pick if your work runs on what is happening on X today; confirm what it finds against a primary source before you publish.

## DeepSeek: free app, open weights

DeepSeek's chat app on the web, iOS and Android is free, with no paid consumer plan as of November 2026. It runs DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with a 1M-token context, a DeepThink mode and web search. What sets it apart is the licence: the model card says "the model weights are licensed under the MIT License," so you can run the models yourself, and the pay-as-you-go API speaks both the OpenAI and Anthropic formats. The catch is data location. The privacy policy says DeepSeek stores personal data "in People's Republic of China" and uses it for training, with an opt-out. That rules out the hosted app for confidential work, while the [open weights](/glossary/open-weights) suit teams that host models themselves.

**Verdict:** the pick for developers who want open weights and a low-cost API, and a capable free app for anything non-confidential.

## Mistral Vibe (formerly Le Chat): chat plus a coding agent

Le Chat became Mistral Vibe on May 28, 2026: "one agent and one licence across work and code." Work mode, the old Le Chat chat, handles conversation, research, scheduled tasks and connectors to Gmail, Slack, Notion, GitHub and more, asking for approval "before sensitive actions." Code mode is a coding agent in your terminal, VS Code, a desktop app or a cloud sandbox, and its CLI is open source under Apache-2.0. Paris-based Mistral AI trains on Free, Pro, Team and Student data unless you opt out; Enterprise is opted out by default.

**Verdict:** the European alternative, and the natural pick for one subscription covering chat and a terminal coding agent.

## Meta AI: already in your messaging apps

Meta AI lives in the Meta AI app, on meta.ai, and inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger and Threads. Since April 8, 2026 it has run on Muse Spark, "the first model in our new Muse series," with Instant and Thinking modes. On meta.ai it generates and edits images, can "search across sources, organize the findings, and turn them into a cited report," and helps with shopping and local recommendations. Meta's April upgrade started rolling out in the US first. Meta AI is a different product from Muse, the personal AI agent app Meta launched on September 8, 2026.

**Verdict:** the convenient choice when the assistant you want is already inside WhatsApp or Instagram.

## Duck.ai: the private option

Duck.ai is DuckDuckGo's way to "have private conversations with 3rd-party AI chat models, anonymized by us." The free lineup includes Claude Haiku 5.5, Mistral Small 4, GPT-6 Luna, GPT-5.4 mini, gpt-oss-120b and Gemma 4 31B. Recent Chats are stored on your device, and DuckDuckGo says its AI features "don't track how you use them, store your prompts, or train on your data." Stronger models, such as Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Opus 5.5 and the GPT-5.6 models, need a DuckDuckGo subscription.

**Verdict:** the best ChatGPT alternative when privacy matters more than features.

## The best ChatGPT alternative for each job

### Writing

Claude. Projects keep your style guide and sources together, and Artifacts and Docs are on the Free plan. [Claude vs ChatGPT for writing](/guides/comparisons/claude-vs-chatgpt-for-writing) tests both on identical briefs, and the [best AI writing tools roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-ai-writing-tools-2026) covers specialist editors. Mistral Vibe is the alternative if you prefer a European provider.

### Research with citations

Perplexity, for inline citations on every answer and premium data sources on Pro. Gemini's Deep Research, on the Free plan, is the second choice for longer reports, and Meta AI can turn a search into a cited report. Treat Grok's live results as leads, since its FAQ warns they can repeat wrong public information.

### Coding

Claude's paid plans include Claude Code, Mistral Vibe's Code mode shares a licence with its chat, Grok's Plus plan includes Cursor, and ChatLLM includes Abacus AI Desktop with a coding agent and CLI, unrestricted on Pro while you have credits left. DeepSeek's documentation says you can use it as "the backend model directly" for Claude Code, GitHub Copilot or OpenCode; the guide to [running Claude Code with other models](/guides/configuration/claude-code-with-other-models) shows the setup. For terminal agents in depth, see the [Claude Code alternatives roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-claude-code-alternatives-2026).

### Several models in one app

ChatLLM puts GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and open models behind one subscription and can route each prompt for you with RouteLLM. Perplexity also draws on several labs' models, and Pro and Max let you pick one, but it is built around cited search answers rather than drafting. Duck.ai offers a smaller set free and anonymized. ChatLLM's subscription also includes the OpenAI-compatible RouteLLM API; if you want pay-per-token access to hundreds of models without a subscription, look at [OpenRouter](/tools/openrouter).

### Privacy

Duck.ai first. Among the full assistants, Claude's incognito chats, ChatGPT's Temporary Chat, Grok's Private Chat and Mistral Vibe's Temporary Chat are excluded from training, and OpenAI and Anthropic do not train on their business plans by default. Keep confidential material out of DeepSeek's hosted app. If nothing may leave your machine, run an open-weight model locally; the [local LLM tools roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-local-llm-tools-2026) covers the options.

### Free use

Gemini's Free plan includes Deep Research, Canvas and Skills, and Microsoft Copilot is free with a Microsoft account within capacity limits. DeepSeek's app is free. Claude Free includes Projects and Artifacts but resets usage every five hours and every week. Duck.ai's free lineup includes Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna, and Mistral Vibe and Grok both have limited free tiers.

### Google or Microsoft ecosystems

On Google Workspace, choose Gemini; on Microsoft 365, choose Microsoft Copilot. Grok is a third route into both suites: it ships add-ins for Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook and a Google Workspace add-on.
