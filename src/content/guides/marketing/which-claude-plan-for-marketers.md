The plan question is simpler than the pricing page makes it look, because every paid Claude plan opens the same doors. Pro, Max, and Team all include Claude Code, Cowork, and Claude Design (in beta as of September 2026); what changes is how much you can do per five-hour session and per week, and on Team, who administers it. So the decision is a usage forecast plus a sharing question. This guide walks it by situation, names the plan, and points to the page that holds the numbers, which move too often to repeat here.

## How limits work

Two facts from Anthropic's support docs shape everything below. Usage is one pool: "your usage of all different Claude product surfaces (claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Desktop) counts towards the same usage limit." A long Cowork run in the morning means less chat in the afternoon. And what burns usage fastest is documented: "the length and complexity of your conversations, the features you use, which Claude model you're chatting with, and the effort level you've selected." The same article singles out two things marketers do constantly. Long conversations "that trigger automatic context management consume more of your usage limit," and "tools and connectors are token-intensive."

The tiers are multipliers on that pool. Pro is the base. Max 5x "provides five times more usage per session than the Pro plan," Max 20x twenty times. On Team, Standard seats get "1.25x more usage per session than the Pro plan," Premium seats 6.25x, and "usage limits on Team plans are per-member, rather than applied to the team as a whole." Those multipliers are the whole difference between the tiers as of September 2026; the dollar figures behind them are in [Claude plans compared (2026)](/guides/getting-started/claude-plans-compared-2026).

## By situation

### Solo content marketer

**Pro.** You are drafting in [claude.ai](/tools/claude) from a brand Project, running the occasional [Cowork](/tools/claude-cowork) task over a folder, building a one-pager in Claude Design now and then, and trying Claude Code on an export. Pro includes all of it. Anthropic's upgrade guidance is to move up when you "consistently hit limits," and at this level of use you will not. Set up the skills in [Claude Skills for Marketers](/guides/marketing/claude-skills-for-marketers) before you spend anything more.

### Small team sharing brand context

**Team.** The moment a second person needs the style guide, the personas, and the past work in the same place, the Team plan earns itself, because a Project on Team can be shared with "Can view" or "Can edit" permissions. One owner maintains the brand Project; everyone else works from it. Team also adds admin tools, central billing, role-based permissions, spend controls, and workplace connectors (Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, GitHub, Microsoft 365, Slack). It requires at least two members. Give a Premium seat to whoever runs the batch jobs and Standard seats to everyone drafting in chat. The surface map for the team is [Claude for Marketing Teams](/guides/marketing/claude-for-marketing-teams).

### Heavy Cowork and Claude Design use

**Max 5x, then 20x if it keeps running out.** A scheduled Cowork task every morning over a large folder, connectors enabled, plus Claude Design sessions that iterate on a deck for an hour, is the most usage-intensive pattern a solo marketer runs. Anthropic frames the path exactly this way: Max 5x "if you consistently hit limits," 20x "if you're on the Max 5x plan" and still hit them. Max also carries "priority access" to new models and features. Start at 5x and let the reset warnings tell you whether you need 20x. If the heavy user is one person on a team, a Premium Team seat is the equivalent move.

### Agency

**Team, with Premium seats by role.** Per-member limits are the point: an account manager's afternoon should not be drained by the content lead's thirty-post batch. Role-based permissions and spend controls let you cap what each seat can use, and a Project per client keeps brand context separated. Enterprise sits above Team for organizations whose procurement requires it; whether that applies depends on your clients, not your workload.

### Anything running unattended in Claude Code

**API credits through the Claude Console.** A nightly script that checks every draft for unsourced statistics, or a Claude Code routine that rebuilds the sitemap and reports broken links, should not compete with your own session limit. Claude Code logs into a Console account with prepaid credits and you pay per token. The founder version of this decision, [Which Claude Plan Should a Founder Pay For?](/guides/founders/which-claude-plan-for-founders), covers the API route in more detail.

## Picking a model

Every plan lets you choose the model, and marketers overspend by defaulting to the largest. Anthropic ships a family with three tiers; match the tier to the task:

| Task | Tier | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tagging, bulk rewrites, meta descriptions, first passes over a batch | Fastest | Speed and low usage matter more than nuance |
| Most drafting, briefs, repurposing, email sequences | Middle | The default for real work; best quality per unit of usage |
| The flagship piece, the positioning doc, the analysis you will act on | Top | Quality is the point; use it for the piece, not the whole session |

The current names, where each sits, and which ones are gated to particular plans are in [Choosing the right model](/guides/getting-started/choosing-the-right-model). For the main tiers, model choice is independent of plan: you do not need Max to use a better model, you need Max to use it for hours.

## The short answer

Start on Pro. Go to Team the day a second person needs the brand Project, with Premium seats for the heavy users. Go to Max 5x if you are solo and scheduled Cowork runs or long Claude Code sessions keep hitting the five-hour limit, and 20x only when 5x keeps running out. Push unattended jobs to API credits. Choose the model per task.
