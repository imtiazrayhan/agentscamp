## Is it really a deal?

Most sale prices are first-term prices on annual plans, and some vendors measure the discount against monthly billing. Against the price you would pay for a normal annual plan, the saving is often smaller. Using each vendor's own figures, verified November 23, 2026 (percentages are our arithmetic):

| Deal | Headline | Against the regular annual price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ElevenLabs Creator | 55% off | $99 instead of $220: 55% off |
| Otter Pro | 50% off | $49.99 instead of $99.99: 50% off |
| Recraft Pro | 40% off | $115.20 instead of $192: 40% off |
| Synthesia Pro yearly | Up to 50% off | $44 a month instead of $59: about 25% off |
| Manus Pro annual | 25% off | $900 instead of $1,000: 10% off |
| InVideo Plus | 60% off | $288, the same as its renewal price: no saving on annual |

ElevenLabs, Otter and Recraft measure their discounts against the annual price, so the headline holds. Synthesia, Manus and InVideo measure theirs against monthly billing. Their deals still save money if you were going to pay monthly, but if you already planned an annual plan, the extra saving is much smaller. Also check whether you qualify: Synthesia excludes current paid subscribers, Recraft excludes accounts with a yearly plan, and Otter excludes existing annual customers.
