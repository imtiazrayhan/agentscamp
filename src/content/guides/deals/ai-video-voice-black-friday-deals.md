## ElevenLabs

[ElevenLabs](/tools/elevenlabs) has the clearest terms of any deal here. Its Black Friday terms page offers Creator annual at $99 for the first 12-month term, "55% off the regular annual price of $220", and Pro annual at $445.50, 55% off the regular $990. Pro subscribers on monthly billing who switch to annual pay $495, which is 50% off. Billed monthly, Creator is $22 and Pro $99, verified November 23, 2026.

The offer runs from 3:00 a.m. ET on November 16 to 11:59 p.m. ET on December 1, 2026, with no code. "New" means you have never had a paid ElevenLabs subscription; "upgrading" means you weren't already on Creator annual, Pro annual or higher at 12 a.m. UTC on November 16. It applies only to purchases on elevenlabs.io, not app stores, can't be combined with other offers, and excludes Free, Starter, Scale, Business, Enterprise, prepaid credits and API usage. If you voice videos or podcasts every month, a $99 Creator year is the cheapest Creator has been, though Starter is still the cheapest paid plan; developers paying for API usage get nothing from it. For how its voices compare, see the [best text-to-speech APIs](/guides/voice/best-tts-apis-2026).

## Synthesia

[Synthesia](/tools/synthesia)'s pricing page says: "Get Pro for $19 with our biggest offer of the year. Save up to 50% on yearly plans." As verified November 23, 2026:

- **Pro:** $19 for the first month and then $89 a month, or $44 a month billed yearly ($528), against a regular $59 a month billed yearly ($708).
- **Team:** $29 for the first month and then $99 a month, or $59 a month billed yearly ($708), against a regular $79 a month billed yearly ($948).

The offer runs from 16:00 GMT on November 23 to 07:59 GMT on December 7, 2026, and must be paid for before it ends. It is "available to new customers and free plan users upgrading to a paid plan only, not to anyone with a current paid Synthesia subscription." The "up to 50%" compares the sale with monthly billing: against Synthesia's regular yearly Pro price, $44 instead of $59 is about 25% off. The first-month price has varied during the promotion, so confirm it at checkout. Synthesia suits presenter-led training and explainer videos; the [avatar video generators roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-ai-avatar-video-generators-2026) compares it with HeyGen and others.

## InVideo

InVideo's pricing page advertises a "Black Friday sale", "Up to 75% off" and, on Max, "Pay for 12 months, get 24". As verified November 23, 2026:

- **Max:** $1,500 for two years ($62.50 per seat per month), then $3,000 a year. Billed monthly, Max is $250 per seat.
- **Plus:** $24 per seat per month billed annually, labelled 60% off. The page says "$288 today for 1 year. Then $288/year", so the sale price is the same as the renewal price: the saving is against $60 monthly billing, not against the regular annual plan.

Starter is labelled 50% off too, at $120 for the year and then $120 a year. The page shows a countdown rather than a written end date; when we checked, it ran out on November 26 at midnight PT, so the offer may change or end then. InVideo also shows different plans to visitors outside the US, so check what your country sees.

## VEED

[VEED](/tools/veed)'s pricing page says: "Black Friday: 30% off annual plans with code BLACKFRIDAY30", above a countdown. Billed yearly, Creator is $12 and Pro is $22 per user per month, verified November 23, 2026, so with the code they work out to $8.40 and $15.40 (our arithmetic). VEED is a browser editor with AI subtitles, translation and avatars, a fit for teams cutting social clips.

## Fliki

Fliki's pricing page says: "Black Friday Sale! 50% off annual Standard & Premium plans with code FLIKIBFCM50, applied at checkout." The pricing page shows Standard at $14 per user per month ($168 a year) and Premium at $44 ($528 a year), verified November 23, 2026, against struck-through prices of $28 and $88; it doesn't say whether those prices already include the code. The page doesn't say when the sale ends, or whether the struck prices are monthly or regular annual prices.

## Suno

Suno, the AI music generator, is running "Extra 30% off annual plans" under a "Black Friday Sale" banner with a countdown. As verified November 23, 2026, Pro is $67.20 for the first year ($5.60 a month) and then $96 a year; Premier is $201.60 and then $288; Platinum is $840 and then $1,200. Billed monthly, the same plans are $10, $30 and $120. That is 30% off the regular annual price, or about 44% off a year of monthly billing on Pro and Premier and 42% on Platinum (our arithmetic). The sale prices show on Suno's pricing page and in the pricing section of its homepage; check the total at checkout.

## Adobe Firefly and Adobe Podcast

[Adobe Podcast](/tools/adobe-podcast)'s pricing page carries Adobe's offer: "Save 50% on Adobe Firefly Pro plan for the first 6 months (with access to Adobe Podcast Premium). Ends Dec 6." Firefly Pro is US$9.99 a month for six months instead of US$19.99, or US$8.49 a month on the annual plan billed monthly, renewing at US$16.99, verified November 23, 2026. Adobe's offer terms limit it to eligible first-time Firefly Pro subscribers who buy directly from Adobe. The Firefly plans page says "Save up to 60% on select Firefly plans for the first 6 months," with Firefly Pro Plus at US$19.99 instead of US$49.99. Adobe doesn't give a time zone for the end date. If you mainly want Adobe Podcast Premium and have never subscribed to Firefly Pro, this is a cheap way to get six months of it.

## Model discounts, not plan discounts

Two video platforms are cutting the price of specific models rather than plans:

- **OpenArt:** upgrading to Pro or Wonder, monthly or annual, unlocks 50% off Seedance 2.0 Fast, Seedance 2.0 Mini, Wan 3.0 and Wan 3.0 Prime, 40% off Grok Imagine 1.5 and 1.5 Lite, and 20% off Seedance 2.0 on openart.ai, through 11:59 p.m. PT on November 30, verified November 23, 2026. Existing Pro and Wonder subscribers who keep their plan are excluded, and the discounts don't apply to generations made through OpenArt's MCP server. Its annual plans are also discounted; see the [image and design section](/guides/deals/ai-black-friday-deals#image-and-design) of the main deals page.
- **[Runway](/tools/runway):** "25% off Kling 4.0 generations until November 30." The page gives no year or time zone, and Runway's plan prices aren't discounted.

## No Black Friday offer found

As of November 23, 2026, these tools showed no Black Friday discount on their own sites: [Descript](/tools/descript), [HeyGen](/tools/heygen), Captions, Riverside, [Luma](/tools/luma), Pika, Cartesia and Murf. Some have standing discounts for annual billing: HeyGen's Creator is $24 a month billed annually instead of $29, and Riverside advertises "Up to 20% off Yearly". [Kling](/tools/kling)'s plans page needs a login, so we couldn't confirm either way. We'll add any of these that launch a deal on Black Friday or Cyber Monday.

Before you buy, check three things on the checkout page: whether you qualify (several offers exclude existing paid subscribers), what the plan renews at after the first term, and whether the code is applied.
