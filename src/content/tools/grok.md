Grok is the AI assistant from SpaceXSI (SpaceX Super Intelligence, formerly xAI). You use it at grok.com, in iOS and Android apps with synced history, and inside X. Its own pitch is "Chat, search, reason, and create — all in one place," and the part that sets it apart is the search: Grok "searches the web and 𝕏 live," so questions about what is happening right now get answers drawn from posts as well as pages.

The company behind it changed shape in 2026. SpaceX announced on February 2, 2026 that it had acquired xAI, and Grok's terms of service now name the operator as SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC. SpaceX completed its acquisition of [Cursor](/tools/cursor) on August 14, 2026, which explains the plan structure: since October 12, 2026 Grok is sold through the X Subscription, which bundles Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium. Marketers who track trends on X, founders who want one subscription for chat and coding, and developers who want API access in the same plan are the natural audience. The [best ChatGPT alternatives roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-chatgpt-alternatives-2026) compares it with the other general assistants.

## Highlights

- **Live web and X search.** Grok advertises "real-time 𝕏 integration for breaking news and trends," so answers "reflect what's happening right now."
- **Multi-agent answers.** "Multiple agents work in parallel for deeper answers on the hardest questions. Each agent shows its work so you can audit the reasoning."
- **Imagine.** "Text-to-image and text-to-video in one thread," with images up to 2K resolution and videos up to 15 seconds; the Super plan adds 1080p video.
- **Voice.** "Natural voice conversations with low-latency back and forth." Grok Voice 3.0 was announced on October 14, 2026.
- **Work features.** Memory, Canvas, custom instructions, file and PDF analysis and 30+ languages, plus Connectors (May 2026), Skills that generate documents, decks and spreadsheets (May 2026) and Automations (July 2026).
- **Office and Google add-ins.** Grok for Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook, and a Google Workspace add-on, all released in June and July 2026.
- **Grok Bot.** Always-on agents that "have their own computer." With Grok Bot 1.0, Team Bots are available on Team and Enterprise plans, and Meetings on Team, Enterprise and Ultra.

## In an AI-assisted workflow

Automations are the feature that turns Grok's live search into a routine: "Describe a job once and Grok runs it on a schedule or when an email arrives." A made-up example for Acme's marketing team:

```text
Every weekday at 08:00, search X and the web for posts from the last 24
hours that mention Acme Analytics or its two main competitors. Group them
by theme, link every post you cite, and flag anything that reads like a
customer complaint. Skip any post you could not open.
```

Treat the output as leads, not facts. Grok's own consumer FAQ warns that it "may at times include in its responses misleading or factually incorrect information based upon that public information," so open the linked posts before you quote or act on them. The [hallucination](/glossary/hallucination) entry explains why live retrieval reduces the problem without removing it.

Developers get more than chat. Plus includes Grok API access and Cursor. The API's flagship, Grok 4.7, is described as "our flagship model for code and everything else," with a 500k-token context and a May 2026 knowledge cut-off. The Grok 4.7 announcement lists Cursor, Grok Build and the API, not the Grok chat apps; the pricing page promises "Latest Grok models" on Premium, so check the model picker in your account rather than assuming it matches the API.

## Pricing

As of November 2026, x.ai/pricing lists five X Subscription tiers, billed monthly in US dollars before taxes: Free at $0, Plus at $8 per month, Premium at $30 per month, Super at $100 per month and Ultra at $200 per month. Free covers "Limited Chat requests" and "Limited agent requests." Plus adds "Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine, and Build," Grok API access and Cursor, with the X Premium checkmark. Premium, marked "Most popular," gives 4x the usage of Plus and adds Grok Bot, the latest Grok models, and cloud agents and automations. Super gives 15x Plus usage with priority access at peak times, and Ultra gives 50x. Team users get Premium, Super and Ultra at the same per-seat prices as individual plans, and third-party model usage in Cursor is not included in any tier.

The X Subscription tiers replace the SuperGrok, standalone Cursor and X Premium plans, which existing subscribers may keep; the announcement says "Nothing changes for existing SuperGrok, Cursor, or X Premium subscribers." The marketing page at x.ai/grok still says "Upgrade to SuperGrok," but the pricing page no longer lists it. As of November 2026, the Grok API lists Grok 4.7 at $2.00 per million input tokens and $6.00 per million output tokens for contexts under 200k tokens.

## Good to know

Training is on unless you turn it off. The FAQ says "We may use your content and interactions with Grok … to train our models," and the switch is under Settings, Data Controls, "Improve the model." Private Chat conversations are not used for training and are deleted within 30 days, and business and enterprise content is not used to improve models.

Grok is deliberately looser than most assistants. Its FAQ says it "may respond with some dialogue that may involve coarse language, crude humor, sexual situations, or violence," depending on the features you choose, which matters if you are rolling it out on shared or work devices.

Expect some naming confusion. Older articles call the company xAI, the iOS app lists X Corp. as its seller, and the privacy policy stresses that "SpaceXSI is a separate company from X Corp."
