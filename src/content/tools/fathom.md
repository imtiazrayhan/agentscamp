Fathom is the notetaker that made "free" its headline. Its Free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcriptions with instant summaries, the opposite of the minute-capped free tiers at [Otter.ai](/tools/otter-ai) and [Fireflies](/tools/fireflies), and its homepage claims use at more than 500,000 companies. It records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, writes a summary and action items, lets you clip and share moments, syncs to the CRM, and answers questions about a call through Ask Fathom.

In 2026 Fathom shipped version 3.0, a redesigned app with bot-free capture and live summaries, and moved its homepage from fathom.video to fathom.ai, with the help center still at help.fathom.video. It suits founders and sales teams who want video recordings and coaching features without a procurement conversation, at least until the sales tiers become necessary. The [best AI meeting note takers roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-ai-meeting-note-takers-2026) places it against the field.

## Highlights

- **Unlimited free recording.** The pricing page's Free tier lists "Unlimited recordings + transcriptions" and "Instant AI call summaries".
- **Bot or no bot, per meeting.** As of October 2026 the pricing page offers a "Choice of bot-free (in beta) or bot capture", chosen through a Capture Mode before each meeting.
- **Two capture modes.** Transcript Only (bot-free) saves no recording and returns a transcript and summary; Audio & Video (Bot) sends a bot that records the call.
- **Ask Fathom and follow-ups.** A conversational assistant over a call, AI-drafted follow-up emails and custom summary templates.
- **Sales tiers.** Team adds global search across calls and playlists; Business adds CRM field sync, Deal View, coaching metrics and AI scorecards.
- **Integrations.** Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion and Asana, plus Zapier, Make and a public API with recording downloads.
- **MCP for Claude and ChatGPT.** A connector at api.fathom.ai/mcp that sees only meetings you already have permission to view.
- **Twenty languages.** Fathom 3.0 supports English and 19 additional languages.

## In an AI-assisted workflow

With the MCP connector enabled, Fathom becomes a source Claude can query across your own calls. A sales lead (a made-up example) might ask:

```text
Pull my Fathom recordings from this week where the customer mentioned
pricing. For each, give the meeting title, the exact quote, and whether
we committed to a discount. Say "no match" if there are none, and do not
summarise meetings I cannot view.
```

Check each quote against the recording before it goes into a forecast. The [AI meeting notes workflow](/guides/workflow/ai-meeting-notes-action-items) covers the review step, and [Claude for sales teams](/guides/sales/claude-for-sales-teams) covers where the approval gates belong when Claude is acting on call data.

> [!NOTE]
> Fathom's Recording Notice posts "Fathom is recording and taking notes, and you consent by continuing." to the meeting chat. Workspace admins can enforce that message for everyone or block bot-free recording entirely, which matters in jurisdictions where silent capture is not lawful.

## Good to know

As of October 2026 Fathom's pricing page lists Free at $0 and shows two figures per paid tier under a "Monthly" and "Annually (save 25%+)" toggle: Premium at $20 or $16 per user per month, Team at $19 or $15 per user per month with a two-user minimum, and Business at $34 or $25 per user per month with a two-user minimum, and we read the higher figure as monthly billing and the lower as the annual rate because the page implies it through the toggle label and its title rather than stating it. Enterprise is "Talk to sales". The page advertises a free trial and a 90-day guarantee on paid plans. CRM syncs on the lower tiers are capped at three users per domain; Enterprise adds SSO and SCIM, custom retention, organization-wide security controls and HIPAA with a signed BAA.

Platforms: Fathom records on Mac and Windows computers, has an iOS app for in-person conversations and a web app for reviewing calls, and as of October 20, 2026 its help center says Fathom 3.0 and above is only available for Mac users, with Windows bot-free recording in a limited Microsoft Teams alpha. Linux, Chromebooks, iPads, webinars, breakout rooms and Zoom phone calls are not supported. Fathom is an independent company; its LinkedIn page still carries the old fathom-video name.

Against the alternatives: [Fireflies](/tools/fireflies) caps its free plan by minutes, is bot-first with a desktop app for bot-free capture, and claims more than 100 languages and a larger integration surface. [Granola](/tools/granola) is bot-free on every platform and keeps no audio, but it is transcript-and-notes only with a 30-day free history. [Otter.ai](/tools/otter-ai) emphasises the shared live transcript and its agents, with six languages and minute caps. Pick Fathom when you want video recordings and a free plan without caps, and either you use a Mac or you accept the bot.
