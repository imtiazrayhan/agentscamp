Unify starts from the opposite end of the problem than most tools here. Rather than giving you a list to work, it watches for a reason to reach out. Intent monitoring runs across more than 40 sources, and when something fires — a visit, a hire, a job post, a mention — a Play runs: prospect, qualify, personalise, sequence, automatically. Its chat agent ships with more than 50 native skills for the parts you want to drive by hand.

Perplexity, Cursor and Together AI are named customers, which tells you the shape of the buyer: technical companies selling to technical companies, where the signal is a better predictor than the ICP filter. Unify has raised a Series B led by Battery Ventures; the vendor's own blog and the newswire release disagree on the date, so we do not print one.

## Can a Claude user connect it?

Yes, and it is one of the widest connectors in the category. Unify's Connectors Directory listing is dated August 2026, is published as "Made by Unify," and exposes **50 tools**. The endpoint is `https://mcp.unifygtm.com/mcp`.

The oddity is that Unify's own documentation does not surface the MCP server prominently. The connector is newer than the docs' emphasis, so the directory listing is the more current source and the better starting point. That also means the 50-tool figure is the directory's count; there is no competing docs number here to reconcile, unlike [Attio](/tools/attio) or [Apollo](/tools/apollo). We make no claim about the listing's verification badge. Anthropic's directory does mark some connectors verified, but its own page says Anthropic "does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won't change".

Fifty tools is a lot of reach for an agent, and the useful first workflow is read-only: ask Claude which Plays fired this week, which signals produced replies, and which sequences are burning credits on accounts that never engage. That is analysis Unify's own dashboards do not shape for you. [Claude for sales teams](/guides/sales/claude-for-sales-teams) covers scoping a connector this wide before you let it write.

## Pricing: the cleanest self-serve here

As of September 2026 Unify lists Free at $0 for three seats, Base at $20 per seat per month with 800 credits per seat, Pro at $60 per seat per month with 2,400 credits and a 14-day trial, and a custom Business tier billed annually.

That is worth pausing on, because six of the ten tools we cover in this category sell AI on a meter wholly separate from seats. Unify does not. Credits are bundled into the seat rate, so a team of five on Pro knows what it is spending and how much automation that buys, without modelling a second meter. The ceiling is lower than [Clay](/tools/clay)'s and the forecast is far easier — which is the trade if your motion is a repeatable set of Plays rather than bespoke enrichment.

Credits are allocated per seat, so the simple lever for more capacity is adding people or moving up a tier. Unify's Base feature list does include the ability to add credits, and Business is sold with custom workspace credits, so a top-up path exists; model which of the two you will actually use before committing a whole team.

## Where it fits against the alternatives

Unify overlaps [Clay](/tools/clay) heavily, and the honest differentiator is the trigger. Clay is a workbench: you build the table, you choose the waterfall, you pay two meters. Unify is a tripwire: the signal arrives and the play runs, on flat seats. Teams that want control buy Clay; teams that want a loop buy Unify, and a few run both.

Against [Apollo](/tools/apollo) the split is data versus signal — Apollo sells you the database and the sequencer, Unify sells you the reason to sequence. Against [ZoomInfo](/tools/zoominfo), Unify is not trying to be the dataset at all; it consumes signals and expects [lead enrichment](/glossary/lead-enrichment) to happen alongside it.

Unify is the closest thing in this set to a configurable [AI SDR](/glossary/ai-sdr) that a non-engineer can actually operate. The [best AI sales tools of 2026](/guides/comparisons/best-ai-sales-tools-2026) roundup places it against the rest.
