ChatLLM is Abacus.AI's all-in-one AI assistant, sold as ChatLLM Teams. Instead of tying you to one lab, a single subscription puts GPT 6.2 Sol and Astra, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2, Gemini 4 Neon, Grok 4.7, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi and GLM models in one chat window. You pick a model from the selector or leave it on RouteLLM, which Abacus.AI says "automatically routes each prompt to the best model for the job." The landing page advertises "100+ Top AI Models," a count the pricing card gives as "100+ AI & Image Models." Abacus.AI is based in San Francisco and was founded by Bindu Reddy and Arvind Sundararajan.

It suits people who already move between [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt), [Claude](/tools/claude) and [Gemini](/tools/gemini) and would rather pay for one app than three, and small teams that want shared Projects and admin settings on the same subscription. The [best ChatGPT alternatives roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-chatgpt-alternatives-2026) compares it with the single-vendor assistants.

## Highlights

- **Many models, one selector.** The FAQ lists "GPT-6.2 Astra, GPT-6.2 Sol, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.2, Gemini 4 Neon, Grok 4.7, Qwen 3.8 Max, Qwen 3.8 Flash Next, DeepSeek v4.1, Kimi K3.1 Pro, GLM 5.3, GLM 5.3 Flash and 20 other closed and open source models," and says new models arrive "24-48 hrs after the release."
- **RouteLLM.** Leave the selector on RouteLLM to have each prompt routed for you, or "create your own custom router." The same router is offered as "an OpenAI-compatible API endpoint," and the help docs say the RouteLLM API "comes with your ChatLLM subscription." See [model routing](/glossary/model-routing) for how routers decide.
- **Abacus AI Agent, formerly DeepAgent.** A general-purpose agent that "builds and deploys full-stack web and mobile apps," works on real codebases, and produces presentations, research reports and data analyses. It can run "on schedules and triggers," and most tasks take 5 to 25 minutes.
- **Images and video.** Image generators include Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2.6, and video runs through Abacus AI Studio with models such as Veo 3.1. On Basic, Studio is capped at three conversations.
- **Documents and data.** Upload PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files and images to "analyze, summarize, and discuss" them, or generate charts from CSV and Excel files. Uploads can run to 2,000 pages.
- **Search, research and Projects.** Web search for current information, Deep Research, and Projects to "Organize Chats And Files."
- **Connectors.** The FAQ names "Slack, Teams, Confluence, G-Drive, GCal, Gmail," and the landing page claims "1-Click Connect To 100+ Services." See [AI connectors](/glossary/ai-connectors).
- **Bring your history.** You can import conversations exported from ChatGPT or Claude: "Importing is available on paid plans and does not consume any credits."
- **Coding.** Code Playground will "generate and run code for self-contained applications" next to the chat. Abacus AI Desktop for macOS, Windows and Linux, which "Combines CoWork, CLI and Desktop Listener," adds a desktop chat app, the CoWork agent and a coding CLI.
- **Apps.** Web, iOS (iOS 18 or later) and Android, with voice mode on mobile. A Chrome extension "requires an active AbacusAI Pro subscription."

## In an AI-assisted workflow

The everyday use is trying one prompt on several labs' models. A made-up example for Acme's marketing lead, with the draft attached:

```text
Rewrite the hero section of the attached pricing page in three variants:
plain, playful, and aimed at enterprise buyers. Keep each under 60 words,
and list every claim in them that needs a source.
```

Send it once with the selector on Claude Sonnet 5.5 and again on GPT 6.2 Sol, then keep the stronger draft, or leave the selector on RouteLLM for routine prompts. Save Abacus AI Agent for work a chat cannot finish, such as building a small internal app or a research report with data analysis. Each agent task typically uses 500 to 1,000 credits, and Basic stops at three agent tasks a month, so regular agent work needs Pro. If you are weighing it against dedicated builders, see the [best AI app builders](/guides/comparisons/best-ai-app-builders-2026) and what makes an [AI agent](/glossary/ai-agent) different from a chat.

Developers can point existing OpenAI client code at the RouteLLM API. [Production model routing](/guides/concepts/production-model-routing) covers when routing helps, and [OpenRouter](/tools/openrouter) is the pay-per-token comparison.

## Pricing

As of November 2026, Abacus.AI's pricing page lists two plans. Basic is $10 per user per month, with the first month at $7, and includes 20,000 credits a month, three Abacus AI Agent conversations, and the desktop coding IDE; the billing FAQ says a $7 first month comes with 14,000 credits. Pro is $20 per user per month in total and adds "Unrestricted use of AI Agent," "Unrestricted use of Coding Agent and CLI," complex tasks and 30,000 credits a month; the billing FAQ adds that unrestricted access holds "with active credits." Pro also lifts Basic's cap on Abacus AI Studio video and adds the SuperComputer, an always-on cloud computer. The page lists no free plan and shows monthly prices only. Credits you buy on top roll over to the next month, while the included allowance resets on your renewal date.

## Good to know

Credits are not tokens, in Abacus.AI's words "these credits are NOT TOKENS," and there is no published per-model credit rate for chat: "you can only see credits after usage." Some models stay available after you run out of credits, but "very heavy use after the credit limit can pause these models for the rest of the day." The allowance is also paced: Abacus.AI says the idea is to spread it across the month, and "You can use the last 25% on the last week of your subscription."

Large files carry their own limit. The FAQ says "using large attachments will trigger usage limits and you will be rate limited," and that if you hit one, "we will automatically switch you to a different LLM."

Some help pages and app store listings still show older model names than the landing page and FAQ, so check the selector for the exact model you want before you subscribe.

On data, the privacy policy says Abacus.AI does not use "customer-provided content (including prompts, uploads, or API data)" to train any model, and that it "maintains a zero data retention agreement with all foundation and third-party AI models integrated into our product." Its security page says "Data is deleted within 30 days of cancellation" and that customer-supplied encryption keys "are not supported today."

You can cancel at any time and the plan stays active for the rest of the month, but "we do not offer refunds based on the model's output or performance."
