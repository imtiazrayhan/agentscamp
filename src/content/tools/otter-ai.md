Otter.ai is the notetaker most people picture when they hear the phrase: a bot called Otter Notetaker joins your Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet call, a live transcript scrolls for everyone who has the link, and a summary with action items lands afterwards. Around that core Otter has built the things a transcript makes possible: chat across every meeting you have recorded, a briefing before a call, live coaching during it, and role-specific agents for sales, SDR, recruiting, education and media work. Its homepage now calls the product a "Conversational Knowledge Engine", which is marketing, but it does say where the roadmap points: the April 2026 release that searches across connected enterprise tools, and an MCP server that lets other assistants search Otter.

It suits teams that want the shared live transcript and accept a visible participant in exchange. The [best AI meeting note takers roundup](/guides/comparisons/best-ai-meeting-note-takers-2026) compares it with the bot-free and free-first alternatives.

## Highlights

- **Bot capture by default.** Otter Notetaker "joins Zoom, MS Teams, and Google Meet to automatically write and share notes", in the pricing page's words.
- **Record from anywhere else.** Otter also records "directly from your Mac or Windows desktop, mobile device, or your browser", with a Chrome extension in the download menu.
- **Before, during and after.** Otter "briefs you before the call, coaches you live based on what's been said", then produces the summary and action items.
- **Botless desktop recording.** The Mac and Windows desktop app adds botless recording for meetings, meeting reminders and automatic recording.
- **Agents.** The SDR Agent lets you "launch instant conversations with prospects, directly through your website", with lead qualification, live demos and booked meetings; the Recruiting Agent syncs with Greenhouse.
- **MCP, API and CRM.** An MCP server on Pro, API and webhooks on Business, Zapier, and synced notes to Salesforce or HubSpot for one user on Pro, five on Business and unlimited on Enterprise.
- **Six transcription languages.** English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese.

## In an AI-assisted workflow

Otter's own chat answers questions about one conversation or across many; the MCP server lets Claude do the same with your conversations as a source. A sales rep (an invented example) might ask either one:

```text
Across my last five calls with the Northwind account, list every objection
the buyer raised with the exact wording, who answered it, and whether the
answer satisfied them. Then draft a follow-up email that addresses the two
still open. Quote the transcript for each objection.
```

Verify the quotes against the transcript before the email goes out, and use the [AI meeting notes workflow](/guides/workflow/ai-meeting-notes-action-items) to keep decisions and proposals apart. [Claude for sales teams](/guides/sales/claude-for-sales-teams) covers wiring meeting data into Claude with the right approval gates.

> [!NOTE]
> Otter's consent mechanisms scale with plan. Otter Notetaker joins as a named participant and posts a chat message linking to the live transcript on every plan; the desktop app can post a notification to a Teams meeting chat and shows a floating recording indicator; Enterprise adds pre-recording emails to all invitees five to ten minutes before the meeting, a recording disclaimer email and a custom notetaker name. Recording law varies by jurisdiction, so check it.

## Good to know

As of October 2026 Otter's pricing page lists Basic as free, Pro at $16.99 per user per month on monthly billing or $8.33 per user per month on annual billing, Business at $30 per user per month monthly or $19.99 per user per month annually, and Enterprise by demo. The same page also shows regional and time-limited promotional prices; the figures above are the list prices. Caps matter as much as price: Basic allows 300 minutes per user per month and 30 minutes per conversation; Pro allows 1,200 minutes, 90 minutes per conversation and ten imports a month; Business and Enterprise are unlimited with a four-hour conversation limit. Otter AI Chat allows 20 queries per user on Basic, 50 on Pro and 200 on Business, and concurrent meetings go from one to three.

Enterprise rows include SSO and SCIM with a 100-user minimum, a HIPAA add-on and custom data retention; security features include TLS, AES-256 encryption for real-time content and two-factor authentication. Otter runs on the web, on macOS 12.3 and higher, on Windows, on iOS and Android, and as a Chrome extension; there is no Linux app. The pages we read do not disclose which language models Otter uses.

Against the alternatives: [Fireflies](/tools/fireflies) is also bot-first with a desktop app for bot-free capture, claims more than 100 languages, and has a broader integration surface and a longer-established MCP server. [Granola](/tools/granola) never joins as a bot, stores no audio and has no minute caps, but limits free history to 30 days and offers no shared live transcript. [Fathom](/tools/fathom) gives unlimited free recordings with video and leans toward sales coaching. Pick Otter when the live transcript that everyone can follow, and the agents built on top of it, are the point.
