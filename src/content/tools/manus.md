Manus is a general-purpose AI agent: you describe a job, and it plans the steps and carries them out. Planning happens in the Manus cloud; the work runs in a cloud sandbox by default, in a dedicated Cloud Computer, in your own Chrome or Edge, or on your Mac or Windows PC. It suits founders, marketers and analysts whose work is research, documents and web tasks rather than code.

Meta acquired Manus on December 29, 2025. On August 11, 2026, Manus told users it would "return to operating as an independent company", a step it said was needed "to comply with regulatory requirements in specific parts of the world". On September 1, 2026, it "formally resumed independent operations" with its founding team still in charge, and four weeks later it shipped Manus 2.0.

## Highlights

- **Several places to run.** Manus Studio, the macOS and Windows app called Manus Desktop before 2.0, runs commands on your computer while "Manus plans and decides each step in the Manus cloud". A Cloud Computer is a paid add-on that "stays on when your laptop is closed".
- **Your browser or its own.** The Browser Operator extension "transforms your web browser into an autonomous agent" with your logged-in sessions; Chrome and Edge are recommended. Cloud Browser runs in Manus's cloud instead.
- **Wide Research.** It "deploys hundreds of independent agents that work in parallel".
- **Automations and Mail Manus.** Since 2.0, "a new email, a change in ad performance, a calendar event, a Slack message, or a Notion update can all trigger a task". Mail Manus starts tasks from emails forwarded to a bot address, and only pre-approved senders can trigger them.
- **Cascade.** The 2.0 agent harness. Manus's own figures, from "one tested configuration", are 23.2% fewer tokens, 28.2% less time and 32% lower cost than before.
- **Model modes and Manus Flex.** Tasks run in Manus 2.0 Max, Manus 2.0 or Manus 2.0 Lite. Manus Flex lets you bring a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI Studio, xAI, Meta, OpenRouter, Fireworks or Modal. It is in beta as of November 2026, and Manus "does not guarantee" how third-party models perform.
- **Integrations.** Slack, Zapier, Connectors for "apps, MCP servers, and APIs", and a REST API.
- **Cue.** A separate app for personal agents. The 2.0 announcement, the latest status we found as of November 2026, says it is in early access and free with an invite code.

## In an AI-assisted workflow

Give each task the least access that can finish it. Research that needs no logins belongs in the cloud sandbox. A fictional example:

```text
Research the five largest competitors to Acme Analytics (a made-up company).
For each, list the plan names on its pricing page, the integrations its site
names, and any product announcements from the last 90 days. Return a table
with a source URL in every row. Do not sign in to any site or fill any form.
```

Use Browser Operator only when a task needs your own accounts: "You must authorize every session, and you can stop the agent at any time by simply closing the browser tab." For local files, Manus Studio has three permission modes: Ask for approval; Approve for me, the default, which "reviews and auto-approves safe local actions" and blocks high-risk ones such as sending private keys to the internet; and Full access. The [human-in-the-loop workflows guide](/guides/workflow/human-in-the-loop-ai-workflows) covers where approvals belong.

> [!WARNING]
> Studio's approval modes control what Manus changes, not what it reads. The docs say "Approval modes limit changes, not reading" and "Commands can still read other files that your user account can open." Run Studio under a user account that holds nothing you would not show an agent, and treat every web page it reads as a possible [prompt injection](/glossary/prompt-injection).

## Pricing

We could not read manus.im/pricing, so these figures come from Manus's help center, which notes that "Everything is subject to what is displayed on the actual pricing page." As of November 2026 the help center lists Free at $0 per month, with 300 credits refreshed daily, one concurrent task and two scheduled automations. As of November 2026 it lists Pro starting from $20 per month with 4,000 credits a month, 20 concurrent tasks and 20 scheduled automations, and a larger Pro option starting from $40 per month with 8,000 credits a month. As of November 2026 Team starts from $20 per seat per month and adds SSO, a data-training opt-out and usage analytics. All paid plans list a 17% discount for annual billing, and the old Basic and Plus plans are now Pro.

Credits meter LLM tokens, virtual machines and third-party APIs, and unused subscription credits do not roll over. Manus refunds credits for tasks that fail "due to technical issues on our end". With Manus Flex, your provider bills model usage directly, Manus covers the temporary cloud sandbox, and media generation, Cloud Computer and other paid features still use Manus credits.

## Good to know

- **Platforms.** Web, macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, with no Linux. Manus Studio needs an Apple Silicon Mac ("Intel-based Macs are not supported") or Windows 10 or 11.
- **Data.** Manus says "Our data is stored in the US and Singapore."
- **Models.** The Manus pages we read do not name the models behind its own modes.
- **A real limit.** Manus's docs warn that in Browser Operator "complex interactions, such as drag-and-drop and multi-step forms, may not work perfectly".
- **License.** Proprietary.

Among the alternatives, [Claude Cowork](/tools/claude-cowork) is now part of Claude and fits work in files and connected apps. [Lindy](/tools/lindy) works from Slack, email and text and waits for your OK on anything with outside impact. [Zapier Agents](/tools/zapier-agents) is moving into AI by Zapier. [Perplexity Comet](/tools/perplexity-comet) is a browser whose assistant works in your own tabs. Manus is the broadest: one product spans a cloud sandbox, your browser and your desktop, and you decide how much it may do in each.
