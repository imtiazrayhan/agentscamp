Peec AI is the self-serve AI-search analytics tool: you add the prompts that matter to your brand, it runs them daily across the AI models on your plan, and it reports the share of answers that mention you, your position in them, and your sentiment. Underneath every metric sits its unit of analysis, the source. In the docs' words, "the basis for all these metrics is Sources: the websites, articles, and other content that AI models reference." The company is Peec AI GmbH in Berlin, run by its three managing directors, Daniel Drabo, Marius Meiners, and Tobias Siwonia; as of November 2026 the homepage says it is "Trusted by 4000+ brands and agencies."

For a marketing team the appeal is simple plan mechanics: unlimited users on every tier, daily tracking, and a fixed number of tracked prompts.

## Highlights

- **Three core metrics.** Visibility (how often your brand is mentioned across AI responses), Position (where you rank against other brands when you do appear), and Sentiment, from daily runs. The docs describe the method plainly: Peec "runs your prompts across AI platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot daily, then analyzes patterns over time."
- **Models by plan.** As of November 2026, Starter, Pro, and Advanced let you choose three from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Naver AI Briefing, ERNIE (Baidu), and Bing AI Mode. Enterprise chooses from all models, adding API-based ones from Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, xAI, Mistral, and Meta, with "up to 26 LLM models tracked." Tracked answers per month are prompts times models times days: 4,500 on Starter, 13,500 on Pro, 31,500 on Advanced.
- **Source and citation reports.** Reports by brand, domain, link, and URL show which pages the models cite: the gap list a content team works from.
- **Prompt volume as a relative score.** "Relative demand for the topic behind each tracked prompt, scored 1–5." It is a ranking signal, not a search-volume figure.
- **Agent analytics.** Crawl insights show "which AI bots hit which pages, how often, and the status code returned," with quotas of 4.5 million bot visits on Starter, 13.5 million on Pro, and 31.5 million on Advanced. The crawlability audit reads your robots.txt and shows which of 40+ AI bots it allows, partially allows, or blocks.
- **Ads library and fact-checking.** Peec records "prompts where AI answers carry a sponsored placement, and which advertisers appear," and checks tracked answers "for claims about your brand against the facts you set": 5 prompts on Starter, 10 on Pro, 25 on Advanced.
- **MCP and API.** An MCP server at `https://api.peec.ai/mcp`, included on every plan, connects Peec to Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, and other MCP clients, with built-in prompts that act as slash commands (weekly pulse, engine scorecard, topic heatmap, campaign tracker). The API is included on Pro, Advanced, and Enterprise.
- **Peec Agent.** An agent that investigates changes in your data and drafts fixes such as content briefs or page edits, connecting to more than 30 tools. It is metered in credits: 500 a month on Starter, 750 on Pro, 1,000 on Advanced.

## In an AI-assisted workflow

Because Peec's MCP server ships with prompt shortcuts, the shortest route is to let an agent run the review. Take a fictional example: Marrow & Co., an invented direct-to-consumer mattress brand on the Pro plan, connects the Peec MCP to Claude Code and asks for a Monday report.

```text
Using the Peec AI MCP tools, compare last week's Visibility and Position
for our tracked prompts against the previous week. For every prompt where
a competitor moved above us, list the Sources the models cited for them,
mark which of those domains we could realistically earn a mention on, and
write a short brief for each. Keep the 1-to-5 demand score next to each
prompt so we work on the high-demand ones first. Do not invent statistics.
```

The briefs land in the content repo and the Crawlability check goes to whoever owns robots.txt. The [SEO content workflow with Claude Code](/guides/marketing/seo-content-workflow-with-claude-code) guide wraps that into a reusable command, and [AI content and search in 2026](/guides/marketing/ai-content-and-search-2026) covers what the briefs should ask for.

> [!TIP]
> Peec's own docs note that "AI responses naturally vary day to day." Read Visibility as a rolling trend, and treat the 1-to-5 demand score as a priority order inside your own prompt list, not as a volume you can compare with search data.

## Pricing

Peec AI has no free plan; the homepage offers a free trial. As of November 2026 the pricing page lists Starter at $95 per month (50 tracked prompts, one project, one country), Pro at $245 per month (150 prompts, two projects, three countries per project), and Advanced at $495 per month (350 prompts, five projects, Looker Studio integration). Each of the three includes three models of your choice, unlimited users, and daily tracking. Enterprise is custom and billed annually: all models, daily or weekly cadence, unlimited projects, custom prompt setup, and SSO. Annual billing is 15% cheaper, an extra model is a paid add-on on the self-serve tiers, and agency pricing is sold separately.

Where it is limited: three models is the ceiling on every self-serve tier unless you buy add-ons, SSO is Enterprise-only, and Starter has no API.

## Good to know

The legal entity is Peec AI GmbH, registered at the Amtsgericht Charlottenburg, with terms under German law and jurisdiction in Berlin, a practical point for EU marketing teams with data-processing reviews. Funding is press-reported rather than announced on the vendor site: Sifted reported on June 26, 2026 that Peec had last raised $21 million in November 2025 and was in talks for a new round at a $200 million pre-money valuation, and no closing announcement was found on peec.ai as of November 2026. Peec is a web application with no native apps found.
