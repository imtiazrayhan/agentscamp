Clearscope is the content optimization tool that has stayed deliberately narrow. Where competitors bolted on everything from keyword research to site audits, Clearscope's core is still the report: you enter a target query, it analyzes what ranks, and you write against a grade and a term list until the draft covers the topic the way the winning pages do. The company's 2026 pitch adds a second axis, "discoverability across SEO + GEO," with tracking of how AI answers mention your brand.

For a content team, the appeal is predictability. Unlimited users and projects on every plan, no contracts, and a term list that editors trust more than they trust a raw score.

## Highlights

- **Optimize with a content grade.** Clearscope's "best-in-class term suggestions" guide the writing and a grade summarizes coverage. Editors collaborate on the same report.
- **Query Discovery.** Enter a topic or a URL and Clearscope surfaces the queries that generate traffic and attention across Google, Gemini, GPT, and Claude, including the "query fan-out" searches AI platforms trigger to build an answer.
- **Prompt Tracking.** Track specific prompts across ChatGPT and Gemini to see when and how your brand gets mentioned, and where competitors are recommended instead. Every plan includes it.
- **Write and Localize.** An AI drafting and editing workflow for first passes (20 drafts a month on both self-serve plans) and a Localize product for locally targeted versions.
- **Protect.** Ongoing monitoring of pages or whole sites so decayed content shows up before the traffic loss does.
- **Google Docs and WordPress.** The support center documents a Google Docs add-on that scores your document in place and a WordPress plugin that links drafts to reports.

## In a content team's workflow

Clearscope fits cleanly in front of and behind an agent. In front: run the report and a Query Discovery pass, then hand the term list and the related queries to Claude Code as a brief. Behind: paste the draft into the report (or write it in the Google Docs add-on) and let the grade tell you what the model skipped.

```text
Attached is the Clearscope report for "customer onboarding email sequence":
the term list with target counts and the top related queries. Draft a
1,600-word guide from ./briefs/onboarding.md in our voice (./brand/voice.md).
Cover every term at least once where it fits, answer the top three related
queries as H2 sections, and mark unsupported claims [CITE].
```

The [SEO content workflow with Claude Code](/guides/marketing/seo-content-workflow-with-claude-code) guide shows how to turn that into a reusable command with the brief as input, and the [Claude Code for marketers](/guides/marketing/claude-code-for-marketers) pillar covers the folder layout that makes it repeatable.

> [!TIP]
> Give the agent the term list, not the grade. A grade invites the model to game coverage; a term list with counts and the question of "where does this fit naturally" produces drafts that survive an editor.

## Good to know

Clearscope is a hosted web app with a Google Docs add-on and a WordPress plugin. As of September 2026 the pricing page lists Essentials at $129 per month (50 tracked prompts, 50 pages, 20 monthly topic explorations, 20 monthly drafts) and Business at $399 per month (300 tracked prompts, 300 pages, 50 topic explorations, 20 drafts, and a dedicated account manager), both with unlimited users and projects, a 14-day free trial, and no contracts. Enterprise is custom and adds custom credits, custom agreements, crawler whitelisting, and SSO. Additional pages and drafts are sold as monthly add-ons. Clearscope says every plan includes priority support, free training and onboarding, and unlimited sharing and exporting. Customers named on the site include Webflow, Optimizely, Shopify, Adobe, and Animalz.

The trade-off is scope and entry price. The product menu has no technical site audit and there is no free tier, and $129 is more than double the entry price of [Surfer](/tools/surfer), so teams that want one subscription for the whole SEO job usually look there first.
