**AI visibility is a measure of how often, and how prominently, a brand or website appears in the answers that AI engines generate: mentioned by name, cited as a source, or linked, across a tracked set of prompts and engines.** It is to [generative engine optimization](/glossary/generative-engine-optimization) and [answer engine optimization](/glossary/answer-engine-optimization) what rankings are to SEO.

The measurement is prompt-based because AI answers have no stable results page. A tracking tool runs a fixed list of prompts against engines such as ChatGPT, Google [AI Overviews](/glossary/ai-overviews) and AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and, in some tools, Claude, then records what came back. The common metrics are a mention rate, which Clearscope defines as "the exact percentage of AI responses that feature your brand"; a visibility score and average position, as Surfer reports; share of voice against named competitors; and citation data, which Otterly describes as "every cited URL, whether it names you, and which rival it names instead." Because each answer is generated fresh, vendors smooth the noise by repeating prompts and averaging, or by rolling averages over recent responses.

Two caveats keep the metric honest. Methodology varies and is often only partly published; Ahrefs' Brand Radar, for example, documents that prompts run through the public web interfaces with no personalization and that its index refreshes monthly. And prompt volume is an estimate everywhere: no engine discloses how often a prompt is asked, so vendors model it, and Ahrefs says outright that weighting mentions by Google search volume "is a modeling choice, not a measured relationship."

Treat it as a trend line: track the same prompt set month over month rather than chasing a single answer.
