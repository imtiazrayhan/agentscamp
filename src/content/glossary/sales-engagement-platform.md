**A sales engagement platform is the system a rep works out of all day: it sequences emails, calls, and tasks across channels, provides the dialer and the templates, and logs every touch back into the CRM.** If the CRM is the record of the relationship, the engagement platform is where the activity that creates it happens.

[Outreach](/tools/outreach) is the incumbent shape, owning the outbound rep's workflow end to end, from sequences and dialer through call recording to deal management and forecasting. [Close](/tools/close) takes the opposite architectural bet for smaller teams by folding engagement into the CRM itself, so calling, SMS, and email live inside the record rather than beside it. [Apollo](/tools/apollo) bundles engagement with its own contact database, and [Nooks](/tools/nooks) specialises in the calling half with a parallel dialer and coaching.

The 2026 shift is that every one of these repackaged itself around agents while keeping the deterministic sequencing engine underneath. That combination is deliberate and worth understanding: reps need to know exactly what will send and when, so the automation stays rule-based while the [AI SDR](/glossary/ai-sdr) layer drafts, researches, and in some products holds conversations.

Two practical notes. Pricing is increasingly double-metered, a seat plus AI credits, so read both. And connector access can be licence-gated: Outreach, for example, requires an active licensed seat plus a specific add-on package before its MCP server will work for you at all.
