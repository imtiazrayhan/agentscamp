A structured interview uses predetermined job-related questions and shared evaluation standards. OPM describes assessing competencies through questions about past behavior or hypothetical situations, with a common question sequence and rating approach. [OPM structured-interview guidance](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews).

## Structure lives in the method

Putting scores into a form is only part of the record. The team also needs a reason each question belongs, an opportunity to demonstrate the relevant behavior, and anchors that describe observable evidence. A vague “strong communicator” rating can remain ambiguous even when every interviewer selects a number.

**Illustrative fictional fixture:** an operations-coordinator task requires retaining unresolved queue records. The shared question asks what to do when two records have the same ID but different text. Proposed anchors distinguish deleting on ID alone, preserving both and seeking clarification, and also recording the conflict and checking source history. These anchors are invented teaching options requiring role-owner review.

An Unobserved outcome records that the interview did not elicit the relevant evidence. It avoids filling a gap with a guess about the person. Human interviewers apply the approved rubric through the organization's hiring process; rubric preparation does not establish a legal or predictive guarantee.

## Prepare and inspect the rubric

The [interview-scorecard guide](/guides/founders/ai-interview-scorecard) starts from approved job tasks and calibrates draft anchors using invented answers. [Workable](/tools/workable) is one product to evaluate for storing the resulting kit.

For local preparation, [job-scorecard-builder](/skills/product/job-scorecard-builder) drafts questions from the supplied tasks. The [interview scorecard reviewer](/agents/product/interview-scorecard-reviewer) inspects unsupported criteria, and [check-scorecard](/commands/product/check-scorecard) checks structure and weight totals. Those checks concern the rubric; actual applicant assessment remains with the approved human process.
